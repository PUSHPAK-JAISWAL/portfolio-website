# Accessibility Statement

## ♿ Our Commitment

We believe the web should be accessible and inclusive to everyone. **Portfolio Website** is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for individuals with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards, striving to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA**.

---

## 🧭 Measures to Support Accessibility

To ensure accessibility throughout the application, we incorporate the following practices:

- **Semantic HTML**: Proper heading hierarchies (`<h1>` through `<h3>`), `<main>`, `<aside>`, `<nav>`, and `<footer>` landmarks.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full keyboard operability across interactive panes, buttons, tabs, terminal toggles, and modal dialogues. Focus visible rings are maintained across all interactive components.
- **Color Contrast**: Gruvbox dark theme palette calibrated for readability and compliant contrast ratios across body copy and interactive UI components.
- **Screen Reader Support**: Meaningful `aria-label`, `aria-hidden`, `aria-live`, and `aria-busy` attributes across dynamic sections (such as GitHub repo fetching and live terminals).
- **Reduced Motion**: Respect for system-level `prefers-reduced-motion` media queries to minimize or disable non-essential animations.

## ⚠️ Known Limitations & Technical Considerations

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility, some areas may have limitations:

1. **ASCII Art Banner**: The fastfetch ASCII banner in the Hero section is decorative and marked as non-essential for screen reader consumption.
2. **Interactive Terminal Simulation**: The terminal component simulates a CLI environment. While keyboard-accessible via the shortcut key (`~` or `` ` ``) and standard button controls, navigating complex command output may require additional auditory feedback which is actively being optimized.
3. **Third-Party APIs**: Content fetched dynamically from GitHub (e.g., repository stars and forks) depends on third-party service availability and response formats.
